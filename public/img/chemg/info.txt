longDescription:
ChemG is a chemistry learning platform built around an AI assistant that guides students through concepts, answers questions, and adapts to each learner's pace. The built-in virtual laboratory lets users run chemical experiments in a safe, interactive environment — mixing compounds, observing reactions, and exploring molecular structures in real time. Designed to make chemistry intuitive and engaging for students at any level.
