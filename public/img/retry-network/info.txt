Retry.Network is a decentralized infrastructure platform similar to Cloudflare. My work covered the client-facing side: a marketing landing page, an internal CRM for managing clients and subscriptions, and a personal account dashboard where users control their services, monitor usage, and manage billing. Built entirely with Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS with a focus on clarity and performance.
