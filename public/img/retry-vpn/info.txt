Retry.VPN is a privacy-focused VPN service with a strict no-logs policy. My work covered the client-facing product: a marketing landing page, an internal CRM for managing users and subscriptions, and a personal account dashboard where users connect to servers, switch regions, and manage their subscription. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
