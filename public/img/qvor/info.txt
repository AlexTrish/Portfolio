longDescription:
Qvor is a personal project — a social platform built from scratch. It combines a social feed with direct and group messaging, real-time notifications, and user profiles. The architecture is built around WebSockets for instant communication and a PostgreSQL database for structured social graph data. Every interaction is designed to feel immediate and fluid.
